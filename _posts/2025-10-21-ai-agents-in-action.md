In this episode of Agile in Action, Bill Ryan, co-founder of Velocity Advisors, joins host Bill Raymond to explore how AI agents are transforming business operations. They discuss how companies are using AI to automate repetitive tasks, improve workflow efficiency, and reduce time spent on manual processes.

From global enterprises saving hundreds of millions through smart automation, to small and mid-sized businesses streamlining onboarding, timesheets, and compliance, this episode offers real-world use cases that show the practical value of AI.

You’ll learn how to start implementing AI agents with minimal technical knowledge using tools like Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT. Whether you're a business leader, operations manager, or part of a growing startup, this conversation will help you understand how AI-powered automation can make your team more productive and your processes more efficient.

**What you'll learn in this episode:**
* AI as a smart coworker
* $500M saved through automation
* No-code agent creation
* Three Horizon Approach to scaling AI
